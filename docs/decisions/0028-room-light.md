## Decision

- **Room lights, observed, never driven.** A room light is a real light in the house (daylight, the overheads, the bookshelf spotlight) with exactly one reporter: a reported light (a source reports it as a dimmer), a measured light (one Sensor reads it), or a fixed light (nobody: always on at full). Central keeps them (`central/room_light/`, pure and source-neutral) and writes their colours and last states as one **room-lights document** on the hub's WALL stream (`contracts/room_light.py`), the same for every Pi; every Node keeps a mirror of WALL, so a Pi keeps the last states while Central is away. The Player reads it through `nodeapi` (a read-only `WallReader`); it never rides the Player's control State.
- **A Frame's lighting replaces its mat** (`contracts/frame_lighting.py`, on `OutputBinding.lighting`): its mat depth, up to four Frame lights, each one Frame's view of a room light (wash, far, close; lux at the Frame with the reference it was read at) and its paper. It is edited live on the wall through Position's trial.
- **The Display's light replaces the sliders** (`contracts/node_picture.py` `DisplayLight` on the picture document): the matched lux (the light at the Frame when the TV's full white matched a sheet of paper) and the panel's black. Contrast, colour temperature, gamma, the signal range and the display's own settings stay as [0022](0022-picture-hardware-and-software-apart.md) and [0024](0024-display-tuning.md) hold them.
- **One physics path** on the Pi (`player/geometry.py` is the scalar reference, `player/native.py` its shader): E(x) summed per channel over the Frame's lights (a close light's inverse square and cone, a far or close light's mat shadow), Lambertian paper ρ = whiteness × (10^−ink + (1 − 10^−ink) × photo), the signal ρE ÷ (0.9 × matched) fitted between the panel's black and white, the fixed highlight rolloff, then dither. Every Frame always goes through it.
- **The highlight rolloff keeps each pixel's colour.** Above the knee (0.8 of the TV's white) a pixel is scaled as a whole by its largest channel's rolloff, so a print under one warm light keeps one colour from its shadows to its paper white; the curve's peak is the paper white's brightest signal on the opening, sampled with every light's cone and the mat's shadow.
- **The Display's colour temperature makes the TV's white neutral, never warm or cool.** The paper match sets it with the White pattern beside a sheet lit by daylight or a 6500 K lamp only, and reads the matched lux at the same time; a warm room is its room lights' kelvin (measured against 6500 K), so the warmth is never counted twice.
- **A Frame light always fits its room light.** Every writer of a Frame's lighting passes one check (`central/frame_lighting/`, its port `FrameLightingCheck`): the Position trial's begin, edit and renew, a Restore from history, and a new Frame, which views the first always-on room light there is, or none. A room light or paper profile a Frame names cannot be removed, nor a light change between measured and another kind (`named_by_a_frame`).
- **Home Assistant's states on Central's hub, Central listening** (superseding "Home Assistant through MQTT", 2026-10-16: [below](#home-assistant-on-centrals-hub-2026-10-16)). A light meter wired to a Pi is read by HostCore (Linux IIO) and reported on its bus line.

## Boundaries (the PR #106 review fixes)

The owner, after the boundary review: "Go" (fix all three design flaws). They supersede what this record said before about who owns what:

1. **The integration edge is drawn.** One adapter package, `central/infra/home_assistant/`, owns everything Home Assistant on Central's side: the references, the 0..255 scales and the listener (its MQTT link, topics and heartbeat are gone, 2026-10-16). The room-light domain speaks only room-light facts through plain named methods (`record_light_state`, `record_reading`, `record_refused`), and who reports a light or a Sensor is an opaque reference `(source, address)` whose address only its source's adapter reads (an entity id; a Pi's player id). The Pi's light sensor records through the same `record_reading`. Home Assistant has its own settings page ("states last heard"); House › Lights shows only lights, Sensors and readings.
2. **A Frame's lighting has one owner.** `central/frame_lighting/` owns the check, the one new-Frame lighting, the trial envelope's limit and which Frames name a room light or a paper profile; every writer calls its one port, which takes the house's one lock (the `room_lights_change` row) shared, while every writer and remover of a room light, a Sensor or a paper profile takes it exclusive first, so a removal never commits between a check and its write. Fleet and the registry never import the house's tables (an import-linter rule). The paper profiles moved from `central/displays` to `central/frame_lighting`. A new Frame records its creation in Position history, so no default lighting is restated anywhere else. One refusal vocabulary per context, snake case, mapped to its status once.
3. **Remembered state never wins over the real thing.** Every reference's last word is kept whether or not a light or Sensor names it, and resolved only when the document is built: a light or Sensor set up later, or a light given another reporter, shows that reference's last word at once, and no rule resets a state. `RoomLightsWall` is the room-lights key's only writer (WALL's ensure only wakes it) and puts the document on every wake and recheck, content-keyed, with no cache of its own. Each Player reports the document it drew with and whether its reader follows WALL, judged by age on Central's clock. The Pi sensor's reading is recorded in its own savepoint, so it never rolls back the host line's record. One supervised LISTEN helper serves every NOTIFY wake.
- **The ports.** Room lights speak through plain named methods, one per question (`central/room_light/ports.py`: `RoomLightReports` `record_light_state`, `record_reading`, `record_refused`; `RoomLightSetup`; `ReportSources`; `DrawnReports`; `RoomLightsDocuments`). The Frame-lighting owner has one (`central/frame_lighting/ports.py` `FrameLightingCheck`). The Home Assistant listener and the Pi sensor's judge are the only callers of the first; the Player line's judge alone calls `DrawnReports`.
- **The Pi's drawn-document report.** Each Player tells Central, on its own bus line (the Player line's `room-lights-drawn` key, contracts/node_state.py; its HTTP route is gone, 2026-10-16) and about every 30 seconds, which room-lights document it drew with and whether its WALL reader follows (`contracts/room_light.py` `RoomLightsDrawn`); Central's show-pipe link records it and `central/infra/drawn_report_judge.py` keeps it. Central judges it against the document on WALL (drawn, behind, not following, not reported) and shows a mismatch on the Frame's Lighting tab and in `GET /v1/operator/house/lights/drawn`.
- **Upgrade data:** every Display gets placeholder light (1,000 lx matched, black 0.03 % of white); one fixed room light "Room" is created; every Frame gets one wash of it at its Display's matched lux times its Display's software brightness (which multiplied the linear picture, so the picture keeps it) and a paper of whiteness 0.9 and ink −log10(black), so its picture equals today's with the other sliders neutral. Mat lights' relative strengths have no lux and are not carried: they are entered again in lux.

## Home Assistant on Central's hub (2026-10-16)

The owner, 2026-10-16: "the goal is for NATS to become *the API*"; "photo-wall components must listen to home assistant to trail light state"; with Central, Central "gets the light state updates and emits a light target for the Players to track"; his answers on the Home Assistant system design's draft-2 page: Q1 A, Q2 A, Q3 A. Read here (not confirmed in his words, an open point): his "light target" is the room-lights document on WALL.

- **Home Assistant publishes its states on Central's hub; MQTT goes.** The Galleria integration for Home Assistant (`custom_components/galleria/`) publishes every light and illuminance entity it has (since 2026-10-25 also every colour-temperature entity, and its birth at one key that is not an entity id: [rows f and g](#colour-follows-home-assistant-2026-10-25)), one value per entity id (`contracts/home_assistant.py`), into the hub bucket `KV_home_assistant`, through **Home Assistant's door**: Central's origin at `/photo-wall/bus/door`, which Central's app relays, bytes untouched, to the hub's client WebSocket (`central/fleet/leaf_bridge.py`, the same relay the Pis' leaves use). The door admits one user, `home-assistant`, in an account of its own that imports only those services from Central's, with permissions the hub holds: publish Home Assistant's states, read Central's state one key at a time, call Central's methods, nothing else, and every reply lands in its own account (`contracts/central_api.py`); every hub user is confined to its own connection type. The MQTT link, its broker setting, its topics, its heartbeat and the blueprint are gone.
- **Central listens.** Central's hub session (`nodeapi.hub` `run_hub_session`, one more task of the worker's one bus task) reads the latest state of every entity at every connect and every new bucket epoch, follows the rest, and hands each to the listener (`central/infra/home_assistant/states.py`): a light's state becomes its word under `ReporterRef(home-assistant, entity)`, an illuminance one its reading under `SensorRef(home-assistant, entity)`, Home Assistant's own unavailable reads Unavailable (since 2026-10-25 a "can't tell" that leaves the last stated value, [below](#colour-follows-home-assistant-2026-10-25)), a key that is not an entity id is refused `unknown_address` (except Galleria's birth key, `galleria`, read as its birth since 2026-10-25: [row g](#colour-follows-home-assistant-2026-10-25)) and a value that is not a state (or a state of the other kind than its entity's domain) `unreadable`. Central keeps each entity's name and when it last heard it (`home_assistant_entities`), for House › Lights' picker and the settings page's "states last heard". Central's room-light setup picks which entities it listens to; the rest are ignored.
- **The scales stay.** Brightness is read linear (level / 255, an open owner point); ~~rgb drives a colour dimmer; kelvin is not read~~ (amended 2026-10-25: a light's colour is read by its colour mode, and colour-temperature sensors are read too, [below](#colour-follows-home-assistant-2026-10-25)).
- **Central on its hub.** Central writes its state as level values in `KV_state_central` (installation: every Frame and who drives it; listening; room lights as drawn; releases; versions; Central's birth and the states epoch), puts every key again after a hub restart, and answers one method, `installPlayer` (a version per Pi, [0010](0010-github-release-sourcing.md#a-version-per-pi-2026-10-16)). Each read and the method call the same Central services the console's routes call (`central/hub_api.py`, `central/infra/central_state.py`).
- **Costs.** The door is open on the home network with no secret: any client there can publish Home Assistant's states or call installPlayer (which reboots that Pi). While Central is away the lights hold their last target; the bucket keeps the newest 1,024 entities, and since 2026-10-25 Galleria's birth is one of those keys: a house past 1,024 entities can lose it to the trim until Galleria next publishes all its states (it puts the birth again each time), so Settings › Home Assistant may say "update Galleria" for a while.

- **No backwards compatibility** (the owner's standing rule): no second document shape, no flag. - **A light that dies for good** leaves its last brightness and colour on the wall, shown as "can't tell since …" (0028 already had this for a reporter that dies silently).
- **Steps, not fades:** each colour change redraws at once; at dusk the sky moves from about 5,500 K to 3,000 K in 100 K steps, and below 3,000 K a step may show beside a print.
- **The daylight brightness bridge** (`sensor.daylight_illuminance`, Home Assistant's Sun Sky brightness percent times 1,000, labelled lux) is not a real lux (R5): it draws correctly because the Frame's window lux is matched against a reading of it; only House › Lights' number is not real.

- **Deferred:** a measured outdoor lux sensor in place of the bridge; a record of which colour was drawn when; fades between colours. **Not planned:** a light following another light's colour.
- **Not run in the delivery that built this:** the bench check by eye (one dusk on the fireplace Frame beside a print, a Home Assistant restart with no jump, the console line against the wall).

**His picks (current choices, revisable), read as one test, "it blacks the Frame":** Q1 A, the Frame draws the last stated value under Central's card (no stand-in light, no fallback picture, no Player lighting rule); Q2 C, a Frame is impeded only when its light at the centre is 0 and a light it names is in doubt; Q3 B, Home Assistant's link closing is a doubt too, but a link that stays connected and goes silent is not (catching silence needs a keep-alive timer, against "It's all event driven", 2026-10-24).

**A doubt, three reasons.** Per reference Central keeps its last word and, apart from it, a **doubt**: why Central can't stand behind that word now and since when (`central/room_light/model.py` `Doubt`, `DoubtReason`; migration 111 turns every "can't tell since" into a "can't tell" doubt). The newest reason wins; a repeat of the same reason keeps its first "since"; a stated report clears it (`central/room_light/policy.py` `doubt_after`, `doubted`).

| Reason | Set when | Cleared when | What to do (Needs attention, from the source's adapter) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **can't tell** (`cant-tell`) | Its source says it cannot tell: Home Assistant's unavailable or unknown, a Pi meter's unavailable | The reference states a value | "Check {entity} in Home Assistant"; a Pi's meter: "Check the light meter on Player …" |
| **can't read** (`cant-read`) | Central refuses a report at that reference as `unreadable` (`record_refused`; `out_of_range` and `unknown_address` stay log lines) | The reference states a value Central reads | Which side to update, by Galleria's stated version against Central's release (`central/infra/home_assistant/advice.py` `galleria_advice`): "Update Galleria in Home Assistant (HACS) to its newest release", "Update Photo Wall (Central)", or, at the same release, "Photo Wall can't read this Galleria: report a bug"; a Pi's meter: "Install the current release on Player …" |
| **link closed** (`link-closed`) | The hub connection Galleria's latest birth names closes: every reference Home Assistant reports takes it (`record_link_closed`) | Galleria reconnects and restates (it puts its birth, then every state, on every reconnect) | "Check Home Assistant and its Galleria integration" |

**The link is judged by connection identity, never time.** Galleria's birth names the hub connection it was put on (`contracts/home_assistant.py` `GalleriaConnection`: the hub's server id and the client id), and the hub configuration lets Central's account hear the door account's disconnect events from the system account. The listener (`central/infra/home_assistant/states.py` `closed`) marks "link closed" only when the closed connection is the one the latest birth names, so a late close of an older connection never marks a newer one. Migration 111 removes the stored birth: an older Galleria's birth names no connection and no longer reads, so until Galleria is updated Central reads no birth (Settings and Needs attention say to update it) and cannot tell its link's close.

**The doubt travels; the reason stays on Central.** A reported or measured entry of the room-lights document gains `doubt` (`contracts/room_light.py` `CentralWords`, or none): Central's two lines for a doubt on the reference its brightness comes from, dated in the house's zone, naming the light and never an integration ("Photo Wall can't read Family Room Lights · since 29 Oct 18:05" / "Its last word: off (26 Oct 15:35) · see Needs attention in Photo Wall"; `policy.py` `doubt_words`). The reason, colour doubts and `since` as a time stay on Central, so a new reason needs no Pi install. A doubt set or cleared changes the document and wakes its writer, as a new word does. The document's size limit and the WALL table grow by the words.

**The Pi judges and shows** ([Player architecture › Central's card](../player-architecture.md#centrals-card)): the Player judges each Frame impeded by the test above and writes `impeded` on its line; Display Host's controller composes Central's card from the admitted run's report; the overlay client draws it when no Pi card shows ([0015](0015-player-base-layer-and-health-overlay.md): two cards, one rule, the Pi's card wins). Central's error is never on the health line.

**On the console.** Needs attention lists every reference in doubt (its reason, since when, what to do, the Frames that use it), from room lights alone, so it shows with no Pi at all; a failed read never says "Nothing needs attention" (`GET /v1/operator/central-errors`). House › Lights says "Can't read since …" and "Link closed since …" beside "Can't tell since …", keeping the last word. The Frame page's Overview shows **Central's error** apart from Hardware: what its Pi last said (`GET /v1/operator/frames/{id}/central-error`: impeded with Central's two lines and the lights, clear, or not reported).

**What still draws wrong or black with no card** (the honest list):

- **A Galleria that stays connected but goes silent** (Q3's option C, not built): its lights keep their last word with no doubt.
- **A link closed under an older Galleria:** its birth names no connection, so Central cannot tell its close from another's. Its unreadable states still raise "can't read".
- **A link closed while Central's hub client is away on the same hub server** (a network blip between Central's worker and its hub): disconnect events are not stored, so no "link closed" is set; Galleria's states restate when it reconnects. A worker or hub restart is not this case: the hub starts under a new server id, and when Central's client attaches to it (`ListenedStates.hub_is`, at its connect and every reconnect) a Galleria birth that names another server's connection is itself proof the link closed, so Central marks "link closed" then.
- **A dark room with every light trusted.** A choice (the black test needs a doubt). The owner's 2026-10-20 words pull the other way: "its entire purpose is to show up when a problem occurs so i never have to ask the question 'why is the screen black when i expected it to be on?'"
- **A dim but not black Frame** lit by a doubted light, **a fraction of a lux** (black to the eye, not 0), or **light only off-centre** (the planned bookshelf cone): Needs attention and House › Lights only.
- **A misconfiguration with trusted values:** the wrong light named, the wrong entity.
- **A light never reported** (adds no light, no doubt), and an **`out_of_range`** reading or colour (the old value stands, a log line only).
- **A Pi restarted while Central is away** with no document held, and **a Pi on an older release** (it drops the new document, shows "Behind" and draws no card).

## Design rules

1. **One writer per value.** A room light's state has one reporter; its colour has one source, chosen per light (amended 2026-10-25: typed, its bulb or a colour sensor, [below](#colour-follows-home-assistant-2026-10-25)). A state stands until its reporter writes again: nobody judges a reading's age, so no two clocks judge one fact.
2. **Light facts never ride the media State, and order never matters.** A Frame light whose room light is absent or never reported adds no light: a total rule, so arrival order needs no invariant. (Amended 2026-10-25: "unavailable" no longer adds no light; the last stated value stands, [below](#colour-follows-home-assistant-2026-10-25).)
3. **One render path, in real units.** Every Frame always goes through the same physics; there is no mode chosen by missing data.

## Costs and what is not done

- Below about 100 lx (a placeholder until the bench measures the Samsung QN43Q8F) shadows lift to the panel's glow and grey steps run short (Q1 B); dithering softens the banding, nothing removes the glow (R7).
- A Sensor reads the sum of every light at its point, so it belongs where its light dominates and must not face the wall; a reporter that dies without saying so freezes its light at its last state (House › Lights shows when it was last heard). Since 2026-10-27 a closed Home Assistant link is marked "link closed"; a Galleria still connected but silent is not ([below](#centrals-doubt-travels-2026-10-27)).
- Until a Pi's first room-lights document arrives (a fresh boot), its Frames' lights add nothing and the picture is the panel's black, **and the Player still tags that draw presented**: the witness claims content the wall does not show for those seconds. The WALL mirror keeps the document across a Player restart. Likewise a Frame whose paper profile LUT is still being fetched is drawn without it and tagged presented. Holding the presented claim back until both are drawn is the alternative (readiness would then wait on the hub); not chosen here, for the owner to weigh.
- One rolloff curve per Frame: its peak is the Frame's brightest paper white, so switching on a light that makes a hot spot brighter also compresses highlights the light does not reach (paper white elsewhere on the Frame drops below the TV's white). A fixed curve, the same whatever the lights, would avoid it but would also compress today's paper white below the TV's white; the owner's "one fixed rule" (Q2 B) is read here as one rule per Frame. Between the peak's samples a brighter point may lie; it is drawn at the TV's white.
- The Display's LUT is not carried by the upgrade (its name needs the recipe Central bakes with): a Display that had a print-look LUT loses that look until a paper profile is chosen on its Frames. Its other sliders (saturation, soft highlights, black floor) are gone by Q2 B. Check the deployed Displays' values before the release.
- Every reference's last word is kept (the newest 256 per source; since 2026-10-25 a reference a light, Sensor or colour sensor names is never trimmed, [below](#colour-follows-home-assistant-2026-10-25)), so a light set up after its source spoke shows it at once; a reference that never spoke reads not reported until it speaks; one trimmed past the 256 reads not reported until Home Assistant publishes it again (on its next change, or the integration's republish of every state at a reconnect or a new states epoch).
- Whether Home Assistant's brightness and rgb are perceptual is not settled (an open owner point): they are read as linear shares (level / 255), kept as built (`central/infra/home_assistant/messages.py`).
- Each new Frame's Position history starts with a "Created" entry.
- Not done: subtracting known lights from a Sensor's reading, learning a Sensor's view, daylight from house time without a meter, a 3D room, glass reflections, the TV's backlight (a plus if found, R7), named light profiles (replaced by R3's additive lights).
